list of resource packs i (aktimoose) made

all of these are made for use on textureldr, just drop the zip file in your packs folder

 

gradient killing

removes gradients from most geometry dash icons. for use in 2.2+. supports both medium and high graphics

showcase video

hiimjustin000.more_icons mod compat ver

 

deltarune explosion

adds the low quality spr_realisticexplosion from DELTARUNE as a death effect. replaces death effect 7. supports all graphics settings

showcase video underneath

 

misc fixes

does some fixes with a few textures in the game

  • makes background of sliderthumb and slidergroove be fully transparent (previously there was a faint white rectangle)
  • fixes a few gaps in the outline of the goldFont (uhd only, issue only exists on uhd)
  • fixes glow on cube 40, cube 54, cube 373, ship 14, ship 96, ship 146, wave 35, and wave 51 to match the respective icons
  • fixes discoloration of teeth on ufo 21 to be consistently white
  • fixes ufo shield on ufos 13, 21, and 39 to connect to the ufo
  • fixes missing gaps in robot 60 and robot 64
  • removed erroneous red line on side of daily chest
  • extra layer on ship 116 no longer has the black lines and they are instead on the primary layer (this makes 0 visual difference in vanilla and only affects alphalaneous.fine_outline)
  • adds a few textures to uhd that are missing them (thanks omenun for the textures)

 

You Manix-ed the mom level.

replaces the level complete text with the "Manix-ed the mom level" promotional image on Google Play's Twitter account.

 

Player Color Zobros Icons (More Icons)

adds the Zobros cube, ship, and trail to the game, but recolorable with player colors. cube is just a simple gradient, and the ship is just a flipped version of the vanilla Ship 17. original red zobros trail is also included.

credits to Zobros for the original icons, recreated by me. supports all graphics settings.

 

Krazyman50 TP Icons (More Icons)

port of the icons in KrmaL's texture pack from like 9 years ago. includes 3 cubes, a ship, ball, ufo, wave, robot, and a trail. supports High graphics only.

 

Boomlings Icons (More Icons)

remake of the boomlings icons pack used in Andromeda's (hacked) verification video for The Ultimate Phase. includes the cube, ufo, and ball, as well as a recolorable version of the cube.

credits to RobTop for the original Boomlings textures. supports High graphics only.

 

SoggyMod+

makes soggy mod better, supports all graphics settings, works on 1.0.8
