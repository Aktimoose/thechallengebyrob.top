Secrets

Ciberbull

Dying as the cube past the ship portal will spawn a recreation of Ciberbull's Discord profile picture

aWTF

Having the wave pass through every Crazy Clubstep star will spawn a recreation of a message from higuy333 saying "aWTF", which was sent during gamerboy80's video "Finding RANDOM Bedwars Parties as a 1 STAR"

Clay Dreams

Dying at 48% by jumping up with the cube triggers the screen to be covered with a recreation of the cover art for Clay Dreams by BMB. This recreation was originally made for a currently inactive level I started in May 2022 named BMB to be used for the endscreen

eBay

This button at 54% triggers text that says "ebay" to appear. This is in reference to when Cyclcrclicly asked me to put "Shoutouts to ebay" in Set 14 of The Hardcore Hour (I still don't know what that is a reference to any further than that)

abacussssss

Going in a complete straight line with the robot on the last blue jump pad in this section will spawn the text "abacussssss win", as well as replacing the ice in a later section with a recreation of their profile picture

5h3i1ah

Flying a little high will replace Choops with 5h3i1ah's fursona, as well as replacing the text under the level saying "Their name is choops" with "Her name is 5h3i1ah"

bomf

Bumping the ground between these two structures will spawn a pusab recreation of bomf's YouTube profile picture

deviation

Entering any of the half block gaps between the spikes and the floor/ceiling in this ship part will spawn a "recreation" of the cover art for deviation by yQwO.

isabelle

Dying to the top spike field will spawn a recreation of my friend isabelle's character dabbing. The dabbing is a reference to a now deleted Play Nintendo video where they answer a YouTube comment by "Brandon Dadoo" saying "can izzy dab"

lenniad

Dying to the last set of spikes will spawn in a recreation of the Twemoji sunglasses emoji. This is as a reference to lenniad's profile picture on discord usually being a variant of a sunglasses emoji
