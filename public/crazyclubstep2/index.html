News Ticker

The news ticker itself is a reference to MTT News from UNDERTALE.

TCBR-BRAND STILL 3 STAR RATED

Reference to the first headline in UNDERTALE, "MTT-BRAND STILL TOP-RATED". TCBR stands for The Challenge by RobTop, which is 3 stars.

SMILE! YOU’RE ON CCTV

Reference to "SMILE! YOU’RE ON CCTV" stickers that exist.
This was submitted by Cyclcrclicly.

GUY FIRED FOR BEING COMPLETELY INCOMPETENT AND UGLY. BOSS SAYS “HE REALLY DID JUST LOOK LIKE THE MOST ABOMINABLE, REPUGNANT, AND VILE BEING” EMPLOYEE CONFUSED

Modified version of the UNDERTALE headline, "LOCAL PLANE CREATES HUGE LINE AT STORE BY SAYING "IT'S NOT LIKE I WANT TO BUY THESE PRODUCTS OR ANYTHING" CASHIER CONFUSED"

BLUE LIGHT IS REALLY GOOD FOR YOU AND YOU SHOULD GET MORE OF IT

This was submitted by Cyclcrclicly I think

MOUSE CREATES COLLAB PART FOR INSANE DEMON, DETHRONES VSC

Reference to abacussssss' gameplay part for what was supposed to be an insane demon

NERD GUY FROM NERD MEME PROMOTED TO COMMISSIONER OF THE INTERNAL REVENUE SERVICE. LAYS 70% OF STAFF OFF FOR USING THE WRONG FORM OF YOUR.

First sentence submitted by Cyclcrclicly, extended by me. Not a reference to anything.

GEORGE NOT FOUND

A twist of GeorgeNotFound to be a missing headline.
This was submitted by Cyclcrclicly.

ACCORDING TO NEW REPORT, GEOMETRY DASH’S EXISTENCE THEORISED TO BE A HOAX. DUE TO A LACK OF RELIABLE OPINIONS, THE CONCLUSION IS HIGHLY SUBJECT TO CHANGE.

First sentence is a reference to an AI generated news headline (and article) that described how North Korea was a hoax.
Second sentence is a modified form of the sentence "Due to a lack of reliable opinions, this placement is highly subject to change." that comes with certain Demon List announcements.

TINY RABBIT TRIES THEIR BEST, RECIEVES TINY APPLAUSE

Modified version of the UNDERTALE headline, "TINY VOLCANO MONSTER TRIES ITS BEST, RECEIVES TINY APPLAUSE". The rabbit is in reference to bomf.

LOCAL GLUBFUB VERY RICH AND FAMOUS AND GORGEOUS

Modified version of the UNDERTALE headline, "LOCAL METTATON VERY RICH FAMOUS AND GORGEOUS". Glubfub is the name of The Keymaster, the creator of The Challenge within Geometry Dash's lore.

FRANCE ELECTS MR. WEIRD AS WEIRD PRESIDENT

Don't know who wrote this or what it means,.,, I Think Cyclcrclicly wrote it and they don't know what it means either so

HISTORIC NEWS TICKER HEADLINE SHORTAGE

Directly taken from the UNDERTALE headline, "HISTORIC NEWS TICKER HEADLINE SHORTAGE", which is the last headline.
